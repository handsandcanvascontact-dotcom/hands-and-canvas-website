The Hands & Canvas Colouring Book

An original creative resource designed to encourage children to colour, draw, imagine, design, and tell their own stories.

Instead of simply filling in finished pictures, children will be invited to become part of the page — adding ideas, changing scenes, designing objects, and turning prompts into something personal.
