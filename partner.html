Partner With Us

Create something your community can make its own.

Schools, community centres, youth programs, and organizations can work with Hands & Canvas on workshops, program series, resources, and creative collaborations.

Ways to partner

Start with the kind of collaboration you need.

School

Bring art programming into a learning community.

One-time workshops, recurring sessions, special projects, or student-led creative programming.

Youth Program

Add a hands-on visual arts experience.

Adapt projects to your age group, schedule, space, and available materials.

Community Organization

Build something longer-term.

Explore program series, Beyond the Studio receiving opportunities, or custom collaborations.

Business / Supporter

Support through funding or materials.

Sponsor workshops, provide in-kind supplies, or help fund core art materials.
