Donate & Sponsor

Help keep creativity free.

Hands & Canvas keeps workshops and creative resources free for children and youth. Your support helps cover the materials that make those experiences possible.

Paint, brushes, paper, clay, papier-mâché supplies, markers, adhesives, tools, and project materials all need to be replenished as young artists create. Donations help keep cost out of the decision to participate.
